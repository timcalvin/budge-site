Support

Have a question, found a bug, or have an idea for Budge? Get in touch.

The easiest way is from the app: open Settings and tap Contact Support. Your email will include the app version, iOS version and iPhone model, which helps with answering.

Or email justincamelcasedev@gmail.com with “BUDGE: Support Request” in the subject.
