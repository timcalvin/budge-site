Privacy Policy

Budge keeps your shopping lists private. It doesn’t collect, sell or share any of your data.

Your Data Stays on Your iPhone

Your lists, items, prices and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Budge has no accounts, ads or analytics, and the developer can’t see your data.

If you use iCloud Backup, your Budge data is included in your iPhone’s backup, like any other app’s.

Reminders

If you import items from Reminders, Budge asks for access first. It shows your Reminders lists so you can pick one, reads the reminders in that list, and deletes them only if you ask it to.

Apple Intelligence

When Budge sorts imported items into sections, it uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Your items aren’t sent anywhere.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Budge with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple handles your request under its own privacy policy. Budge itself doesn’t send your data anywhere.

Contacting Support

If you email support, the message is sent from your own email account. It includes the app version, iOS version and iPhone model, which you can see before you send it. Your email is used only to answer you.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the Budge website, with a new date.
