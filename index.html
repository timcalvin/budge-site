About Budge

Budge is a shopping list with a budget built in.

Make a list, set what you want to spend, and check items off as you shop. Enter each price as you go, and Budge shows how much you have left, so you know you’re on track before you reach the checkout.

What You Can Do

Sort items into sections, add coupons and sales tax, and buy things by weight.

Mark the items you buy every trip as recurring, and see a projected total for what’s still on your list.

Add items and check your budget with Siri and Shortcuts, or import a list from Reminders.

Your lists sync with iCloud, so they’re the same on every iPhone you use.
